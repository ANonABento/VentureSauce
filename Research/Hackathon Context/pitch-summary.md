# VentureSauce Pitch (as sent for Sense Check 1)

Source: `Sources/Filing Delta pitch (Sense Check 1).pdf` (working name "Filing Delta")

## Slide 1: Hook
"Private raises reach the SEC before they reach the press." VentureSauce is a paid network that alerts investors to private rounds the day they're filed with the SEC. It pairs each round with live web data and with notes from scouts whose calls are scored against the filings.

## Slide 2: What's broken today
| | Today | VentureSauce |
|---|---|---|
| **Late** | Databases update after the press release, weeks after the round closed | Alerts the day a Form D hits EDGAR, while extensions and pro-rata are still open |
| **Expensive** | About $30K a seat a year; PitchBook alone made $672M in 2025 | $49–$499 a month, built for small funds, angels and scouts |
| **Unverified** | The best tips move through private group chats, with no record of who was right | Every claim links to the filing and the web page; scouts carry a public hit rate |

## Slide 3: Who benefits, and pricing
- **Venture funds:** reach founders while the round is still open ("Daytona's filing showed $11.7M still unsold").
- **Angels and scouts:** publish scored deal notes to paying subscribers, so a track record becomes provable.
- **Founders:** opt in to share their round with verified investors and see who's tracking them.
- **Everyone else:** operators, recruiters and sellers learn who just raised before budgets and hiring open up.
- **Tiers:** Alerts $49/mo (same-day Form D) · Delta reports $199/mo (filing vs. live web, both cited) · Scout network $499/mo (scored deal notes before the round)
- **CTA:** Join the beta. The first 25 funds get 30 days free.
