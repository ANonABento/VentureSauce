# Hackathon Brief: SenseAI "Talk to Users" (2026-10-10)

Source: `Sources/Sense Check Guide.pdf`

## The challenge
1. **Pick a theme:** *Save time* (a task that eats their time), *Build trust* (AI they'd rely on without checking every output), or *Catch what humans miss* (mistakes that slip through today).
2. **Pick a finance user:** Accountants/CPAs · Banking, lending & compliance · Financial analysts & FP&A · Finance ops & bookkeeping · **Investment, markets & trading** · Wealth & financial advisors.

VentureSauce's core ICP (VCs, angels, scouts) sits in **Investment, markets & trading**. Secondary fits: **Banking & lending** (venture debt and startup bankers prospect newly funded companies) and **Wealth advisors** (newly funded founders and execs).

## What judges look for
1. **Creativity:** a fresh take, not the obvious build.
2. **Real pain:** a problem finance pros feel, *in their own words*.
3. **Hidden problem:** something no one sees unless they asked a user.
4. **User feedback:** what you changed after testers spoke, and why.
5. **Works live:** demo the real product, not slides.
6. **Bring the receipts:** the tester quote that changed your build.

## Sense Checks (SenseAI's MCP tool)
- 3 checks per team, 3 testers per check, 1 shared team token. Each launch uses one check; edits before launch are free.
- Testers are **finance professionals**. They get a text, reply YES, and an AI interviewer calls them for 10–15 minutes. Calls under 7 minutes don't count.
- The tester hears your product description **word for word**, so describe the product rather than giving instructions. Testers never see the team name.
- Results come back in about an hour: a summary and quotes per tester.
- Blocks: **1** (12–1 PM) test the idea, 50% research / 50% product. **2** (3–4 PM) show what you built: link or 1–3 screenshots plus a one-liner, 20/80. **3** (6–7 PM) ask your own question, 20/80.
- Tips: ask open questions ("What would stop you from using this?"), and hide keys and client data in screenshots.
