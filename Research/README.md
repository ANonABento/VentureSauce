# VentureSauce Research

Research hub for **VentureSauce**, our build for the SenseAI **Talk to Users** hackathon (2026-10-10).

> The Sense Check 1 deck called the product "Filing Delta". That was a working name; the project is **VentureSauce**.

> **Pivot (see `docs/PLAN.md`):** VentureSauce is now a Chrome side panel. Drop in a pitch deck and get a claim-by-claim truth table checked against Form D, LinkedIn and the press. The Form D research here feeds directly into that build. Surya owns the Research role (`docs/roles/research.md`).

## Original one-line pitch (Sense Check 1)
Private raises reach the SEC before they reach the press. VentureSauce is a paid network that alerts investors to private rounds the day the Form D is filed. Each alert comes with live web data (Bright Data) and notes from scouts whose calls are scored against the filings.

## Folders
| Folder | What's in it |
