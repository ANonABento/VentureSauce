Legora (Legora, Inc.) - public claims from the company's own announcement (March 10, 2026). Real company; these are its published statements, not a deck.

- Legora raised $550 million at a $5.55 billion valuation in a Series D funding round, led by Accel, with participation from existing investors Benchmark, Bessemer Venture Partners, General Catalyst, ICONIQ, Redpoint Ventures, and Y Combinator.
- Over the past year, Legora has grown from 40 to 400 team members across Stockholm, London, New York, Denver, Sydney, and Bengaluru.
- Max Junestrand is CEO and Co-Founder of Legora.
